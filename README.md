* Understanding Late-Decider Voting Shifts in the 2023 Dutch General Election
Master thesis project — Klasietha Iedema, Tilburg University, Data Science & Society
(supervisor: dr. Michał Klincewicz)

Research question: To what extent does political cynicism predict late-decider voting shifts in
the 2023 Dutch general election?

* Dataset
Source: Voogd, R., Jacobs, K., Lubbers, M., & Spierings, N. (2023). *Dutch
Parliamentary Election Study 2023 (DPES/NKO 2023): Research description and
codebook*. Nijmegen: SKON. https://doi.org/10.17026/SS/D62YTH

* Files
`EDA_final_thesis.py` Exploratory data analysis: cynicism index construction + reliability, target variable construction, missingness, demographics, correlations, interaction/validation checks

> (more files to be added as other stages are completed)
